🔒 Privacy policy

How GRIP AI Arm treats your information. Last updated: 20 November 2026

The short version

GRIP AI Arm is made for kids. We only count how many people visit and which pages they use, so we can make the app better. We use no cookies, we do not save your IP address, and we never collect your name or anything you do in the app. Nothing is used for ads.

🚫 What we never collect

  • Names, emails, accounts or passwords (there are none)
  • Your IP address, or cookies of any kind
  • Photos, camera video, or anything you type or choose in the app
  • Anything for ads, or anything that follows you to other websites

📊 Counting visits

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly visit counter. It only keeps totals: which pages were opened, browser and screen size, country, and which site sent people here. It uses no cookies, does not save IP addresses, and cannot tell who anyone is. It is never used for ads. Only the page address is sent, never anything you type.

💾 Your data in the app

Demos, brains, calibrations and progress stay in your browser on your own computer, and you can delete them in ⚙️ Settings. The camera is off until you switch it on, and the video is never saved or sent.

⚖️ Children's privacy laws

We do not ask for names, emails or accounts, and we do not try to find out who anyone is.

💬 Questions

Ask a grown-up to contact the Wizkidz AI team through github.com/wizkidzai.
