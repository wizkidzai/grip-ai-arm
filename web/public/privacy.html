Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

GRIP AI Arm is a free website where kids learn robotics. You can teach a robot arm by showing it, train its AI brain, and code it with blocks. It was made by Wizkidz AI for kids, with help from adult coaches.

This page explains what information GRIP AI Arm uses, why, and who can see it.

The short version
  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email, or phone number.
  • The camera is off until you switch it on, and the video is never saved or sent.
  • Your demos, brains, and progress stay on your own computer.
  • We count visits with a tool that uses no cookies and doesn't save IP addresses.
  • We don't sell your information, and we don't show ads.

1. Camera

GRIP AI Arm asks to use your camera only when you switch it on, and you can say no. The picture is looked at on your computer to see where your hand or the arm is. It is never recorded, saved, or uploaded.

2. Your work on your computer

Demos, brains, calibrations, and progress are saved in your browser's storage on your own computer. This information stays there. You can delete all of it, or just the parts you pick, in ⚙️ Settings, or by clearing this website's data in your browser settings.

3. Ms. Nova, the AI teacher

Ms. Nova works only if a grown-up allows her once. She talks about robotics and STEM, and she tells kids to talk to a trusted adult about anything serious.

  • Names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, and school names are removed from your message before the AI sees it.
  • By default the AI runs on the same computer, so nothing leaves it. If a grown-up sets her up to use Claude from Anthropic instead, your message (with personal details removed) is sent to Anthropic to get an answer.
  • Her voice is made on the same computer too.
  • If you speak to her with the 🎤 button, Chrome sends the sound to Google to turn it into text. Typing works without it.

4. The robot arm

When you connect a real arm, the app talks to it over USB on your computer. Nothing about the arm is sent to us. Every move goes through safety limits, and the STOP button (or the Esc key) is always there.

5. The Focus Helper (optional)

If a grown-up installs the GRIP Focus Helper extension, Ms. Nova can ask for a distracting tab to be closed in class. It reports only the site name, works only with GRIP pages, and stores nothing.

6. Website analytics

We use GoatCounter to count how many people visit GRIP AI Arm, which pages they use, what kind of browser and screen they have, their country, and which website sent them here. This helps us make the app better.

  • GoatCounter uses no cookies and doesn't save anything on your device.
  • It doesn't save your IP address. It only keeps totals, like "40 visits to this page today."
  • It can't tell who you are, and it doesn't follow you to other websites. We don't use it for ads.
  • Only the page address is sent. We never send your camera, your saved work, or anything you type.
  • If your browser blocks trackers, it may block GoatCounter too. That's fine: GRIP AI Arm still works.

7. Other services we use

Like most websites, GRIP AI Arm loads from other companies. When your browser loads the app, those companies can see basic technical details, like your IP address and browser type.

  • GitHub hosts the website.
  • GoatCounter counts visits (see section 6).

8. Children

GRIP AI Arm is made for kids. It works without names, emails, or accounts, and we don't try to find out who anyone is. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are under 13, please use GRIP AI Arm with a parent, guardian, or teacher. If you think a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your choices

  • Say no to camera access, or keep it switched off.
  • Delete your saved work in ⚙️ Settings.
  • Clear this website's data in your browser to delete everything saved on your computer.
  • Use a tracker blocker in your browser to stop analytics.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date at the top.

11. Contact us

Questions? Ask a grown-up to open an issue on our GitHub page. Please don't post personal information there, because GitHub issues are public.
